VideoFlex Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: [today's date]

VideoFlex Pro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Storage

All settings (speed, volume, EQ, presets, keybinds) are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to any server.

Permissions

Permissions are used only to control video playback on the active tab, save settings locally, and manage tabs for the emergency feature. No browsing history is collected or stored.

Third Parties

This extension has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.
